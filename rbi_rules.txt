RBI Caution Directive (Press Release 2021-2022/1789): Banks, NBFCs, or e-wallet providers never ask customers for confidential details like user ID, login password, OTP, debit/credit card PIN, CVV, or expiry date over phone calls or SMS.
RBI KYC Updation Safety Rule: Any phone call or email threatening immediate blocking or suspension of your account on the pretext of non-updation of KYC is a fraud modus operandi. Legitimate KYC updates must only be accessed through official bank websites or physical branches.
RBI Customer Liability Guidelines (Master Direction DA.1/DA.3): In case of an unauthorized or fraudulent electronic banking transaction, customers must notify their bank immediately and lodge a complaint on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal or National Cyber Crime Helpline (1930) at the earliest to limit financial liability.
RBI Remote Access & APK Warning: Members of the public are advised never to download unknown apps, third-party APKs, or remote control applications on mobile devices upon request by unknown callers, as these allow fraudsters secret access to confidential bank data.
RBI Digital Payment Rule (Directions 2021): Transactions involving the receipt of money into an account never require scanning barcodes, entering a UPI PIN, or entering an MPIN. UPI PIN is strictly required only for sending/debiting money.
